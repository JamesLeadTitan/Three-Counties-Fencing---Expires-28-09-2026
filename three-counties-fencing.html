About Us

Family Run Since 2011, Across Three Counties

We are a family run firm offering a full range of quality work at an affordable price. Since 2011 we have been installing and repairing fencing for domestic, agricultural and commercial clients, and we take as much care over the quotation and the tidy up as over the fence itself.

Durapost approved installer, working for clients including the NHS, Duchy of Cornwall Farms and Barratt Homes. Every quotation is free and comes with no obligation.
